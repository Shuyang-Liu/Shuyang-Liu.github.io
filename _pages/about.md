I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/), advised by Prof. [Reyhaneh Jabbarvand](https://reyhaneh.cs.illinois.edu/). I obtained my B.S. degree from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://english.hust.edu.cn/) in 2024 under the guidance of Prof. [Ming Wen](https://mingwen-cs.github.io/). During my undergraduate studies, I also had the opportunity to conduct research at Columbia University, advised by Prof. [Kexin Pei](https://sites.google.com/site/kexinpeisite/) and Prof. [Suman Jana](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~suman/). I am grateful to all my advisors for their invaluable guidance and support throughout my academic journey.

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering, with a particular focus on understanding and improving coding agents toward reliable and efficient agentic software systems.
